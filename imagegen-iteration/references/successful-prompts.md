# Successful Image Prompts

Use these as pattern references, not as templates to copy verbatim.

## Convention Cosplay Mood

```text
Realistic photo-style portrait of an original young woman cosplayer at an anime convention, dressed as a cute but dangerous near-future bounty hunter inspired by modern sci-fi anime. Half-body composition, high-quality handmade cosplay: white and red tactical jacket, black bodysuit, utility straps, compact prop carrying case over shoulder, safe orange-tipped prop pistol held low, subtle painted synthetic-body seams at wrist and neck, small cheek bandage makeup, short pale-blonde wig with warm orange tint, sweet face with a calm dangerous stare. Convention hall background with booth lights, blurred attendees, posters and LED signs out of focus, realistic camera flash mixed with soft neon reflections, shallow depth of field, detailed fabric texture, believable cosplay materials, not grimdark, cute yet dangerous mood, no readable text, no logo, vertical portrait 3:4
```

Note: the prop choice later caused moderation friction. Reuse the convention,
costume, and mood structure, but avoid realistic firearm-like props.

## Delayed Editorial Metaphor

Use a compact hierarchy: first read, delayed read, relationship, mood, exclusions.

First read: a monumental science-fiction sanctuary where three small figures have
confined a white sphere inside a delicate ring-shaped reliquary. Delayed read: after
two or three seconds, incomplete dark architectural arcs and an eclipsed circle
above resolve into one enormous eye watching them. Relationship: the sphere is the
fragment the figures believe they control; the eye is the larger being that allowed
them to see it. Strict vertical symmetry, sacred scale, black and bone-gold
chiaroscuro, solemn before unsettling. The eye must be absent at thumbnail size but
recognizable at full size. No text, real religious symbols, screens, code, robots,
or extra hidden anomalies.

Why it works: it gives the renderer one immediate focal point and one delayed
reveal, then states the narrative link between them. If the eye disappears, adjust
one recognition cue. If it becomes emblem-like, break its outline rather than
adding more negative instructions.

## Levitating Prop Mechanics

```text
Edit the provided realistic Virgin Punk Ubu cosplay portrait. Preserve the same cosplayer identity, cute mysterious dangerous gaze into the camera, orange braided wig, red contacts, black neck collar, bright cyan-blue jacket with ring/chain details, black dress, hard carrying case, convention hall background, and realistic photo-style cosplay lighting. Replace the current hand prop interaction with a levitating official-style high-performance boomerang / collapsible chakram prop above her right palm. Her right hand is raised near chest level, palm facing upward, fingers slightly curled as if controlling magnetic levitation. The prop hovers 5-8 cm above her palm and does not touch the hand. Match the official silhouette: black hollow circular ring, teal-green illuminated trim lines, central top mechanical module, two short horn-like folded blade fins at upper left and upper right. Important angle: the ring plane is tilted about 35 degrees toward the camera, so the hollow center, top module, and both horn-like fins are clearly visible; not side-on and not flat icon-like. Add teal glow reflecting on her palm, fingertips, jacket collar, and a faint controlled energy field under the ring, with slight circular motion light trail suggesting slow spin. It should read as a dangerous fictional sci-fi cosplay weapon, not a toy, not a UFO, not a gun, not a knife. No blood, no attack pose, no throwing pose, no blade pointed at viewer, no readable text, no logo, anatomically correct hand.
```

## Micro-Expression Edit

```text
Edit the current Luye rainy rooftop railing portrait. Preserve the same rooftop railing pose, body position, hair, outfit, rain, distant city bokeh, lighting, framing, and no visible metal powers. Refine only her eyes and micro-expression: her gaze is no longer blank, sleepy, dreamy, or merely sad; it is locked onto a distant trace below the rooftop. Eyes slightly narrowed with clear focus, lower eyelids subtly tense, a very slight controlled frown with gentle pressure between the brows, flat calm mouth. She looks like she has recognized something dangerous and familiar but chooses not to act yet. Keep the emotion restrained and character-faithful: perception team leader on duty, guarded, alert, old hurt held under control, cute but cool. Do not change her face shape, outfit, pose, background, or add powers. Avoid angry glare, exaggerated frown, wide eyes, sleepy sadness, seductive look, beauty filter, glamour makeup, V-shaped jaw, sharp chin, metal wires, weapons, text, logos, distorted hands, extra fingers.
```
